Grade 12 Hub Community Rules

Grade 12 Hub is a learning community for Grade 12 learners. By using the app, you agree to use it responsibly and respectfully.

1. Respect other learners, teachers, creators and moderators.

2. No bullying, harassment, threats, hate speech or intimidation.

3. Do not send inappropriate sexual content, nude images or other inappropriate material.

4. Do not share phone numbers, private contact information, passwords or another person's private information.

5. Do not use abusive or seriously inappropriate language.

6. Do not spam, scam, impersonate another person or deliberately disrupt study groups.

7. Study groups must be used for educational and appropriate learner-to-learner discussion.

8. Do not share harmful or illegal content.

9. Do not attempt to bypass subject or group access restrictions.

10. Inappropriate content may be blocked before it becomes visible.

11. Learners can report inappropriate messages or users.

12. Violations may result in a warning, temporary restriction, removal from a group or account blocking. Serious violations may result in immediate account action.

13. Grade 12 Hub may update these rules when necessary to keep the learning community safe.
