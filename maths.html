1. Statistics

Statistics involves collecting, analysing and interpreting data.

  • Mean
  • Median
  • Mode
  • Quartiles
  • Percentiles
  • Variance
  • Standard deviation
  • Box-and-whisker diagrams
  • Scatter plots

2. Analytical Geometry

Analytical geometry uses coordinates and equations to solve geometric problems.

  • Distance between two points
  • Midpoint
  • Gradient
  • Equation of a straight line
  • Parallel lines
  • Perpendicular lines
  • Inclination
  • Circle geometry
Gradient: m = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁)
Distance: AB = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]

3. Trigonometry

Trigonometry deals with relationships between angles and sides and the properties of trigonometric functions.

  • Trigonometric ratios
  • Trigonometric identities
  • Reduction formulae
  • Compound angles
  • Double angles
  • Trigonometric equations
  • Trigonometric graphs
  • General solutions
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
sin(A ± B) = sinA cosB ± cosA sinB

  • Circle geometry

Measurement involves calculating lengths, areas, volumes and related quantities.

  • Volume
  • Circles
  • Three-dimensional shapes
